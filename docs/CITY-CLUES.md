# Progressive city clues

Each puzzle has a `hints` array: geographic context first (two incorrect guesses),
then a distinctive landmark or cultural feature (four incorrect guesses).
The clues describe the city, not necessarily a feature visible inside the crop.
They are separate from the post-game fun fact. NYC's experimental crops share its
clues. References checked October 25, 2026.

- **Paris:** Seine in northern France → the Louvre and its pyramid.
  [Louvre: map, entrances, and directions](https://www.louvre.fr/en/visit/map-entrances-directions?tab=list),
  [RATP: visiting the Louvre](https://www.ratp.fr/en/visiting-paris/places/louvre).
- **Tokyo:** eastern Honshu → Shibuya's scramble crossing.
  [GO TOKYO: Shibuya Scramble Crossing](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/78/index.html).
- **New York City:** US Atlantic coast → the Statue of Liberty in its harbor.
  [National Park Service: Statue of Liberty facts](https://home.nps.gov/stli/learn/statue-of-liberty-facts.htm).
- **Barcelona:** Spain's Mediterranean coast → Gaudí's Sagrada Família.
  [Catalan Cultural Heritage: Sagrada Família](https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/en/collection/sagrada-familia).
- **Sydney:** Australian east coast and natural harbor → Bondi Beach.
  [Destination NSW: Bondi](https://www.sydney.com/destinations/sydney/sydney-east/bondi),
  [Destination NSW: Sydney Harbour](https://www.sydney.com/destinations/sydney/sydney-city/sydney-harbour).
- **Boston:** New England → the Freedom Trail and the American Revolution.
  [National Park Service: Walk the Freedom Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/walk-the-freedom-trail.htm).
- **Vancouver:** Canadian Pacific coast near the US border → Gastown's steam clock.
  [Destination Vancouver: Gastown](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/neighbourhoods/gastown).
- **Buenos Aires:** Río de la Plata estuary → Casa Rosada and Argentina's presidential office.
  [Buenos Aires Tourism: Casa Rosada](https://turismo.buenosaires.gob.ar/en/atractivo/casa-rosada).
- **Kuala Lumpur:** Peninsular Malaysia → the PETRONAS Twin Towers' skybridge.
  [PETRONAS Twin Towers: design and structure](https://www.petronastwintowers.com.my/the-towers/design-structure/).
