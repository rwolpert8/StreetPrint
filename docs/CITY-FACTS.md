# City fun fact sources

The player-facing text lives in each puzzle's `reveal` field in `puzzles.json`.
The game displays it under **Did you know?** after the round ends. Keep facts
short and paraphrased, and record a reliable source here when adding or changing
one. Sources checked on October 24, 2026. Draft puzzles also have facts ready.

- **Paris (001):** Pont Neuf's name means “New Bridge,” yet it is Paris's oldest surviving bridge. [City of Paris: Paris and its bridges](https://www.paris.fr/pages/paris-et-ses-ponts-toute-une-histoire-7466).
- **Tokyo (002):** Edo was renamed Tokyo in 1868; Tokyo means “Eastern Capital.” [National Archives of Japan: renaming Edo](https://www.archives.go.jp/exhibition/digital/henbou/contents/03.html), [Japan Tourism Agency: Emperor Meiji arrives in Tokyo](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/001998615.pdf).
- **New York City (003):** The 1811 grid took about 60 years to build to 155th Street. [Museum of the City of New York: Building the Grid](https://thegreatestgrid.mcny.org/building-the-grid).
- **Barcelona (004):** Cerdà's Eixample blocks have corners cut at 45 degrees to ease movement. [Turisme de Barcelona: the Cerdà Plan](https://www.barcelonaturisme.com/newsletter/0609/bcn_newsletter_eng_print.html).
- **Sydney (005):** The Opera House's roof geometry derives from a sphere. [Sydney Opera House: The spherical solution](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/the-spherical-solution).
- **Boston (006):** Back Bay was tidal water before nineteenth-century filling created land for the neighborhood. [City of Boston: Back Bay Architectural District](https://content.boston.gov/historic-district/back-bay-architectural-district).
- **Vancouver (007):** Stanley Park opened in 1888 when the city's population was 6,065. [City of Vancouver: Story of Stanley Park](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park-story.aspx?mgid=18058).
- **Buenos Aires (008):** El Ateneo Grand Splendid became a bookstore in 2000, preserving the theater's dome and repurposing the stage for a cafe. [Buenos Aires Tourism: El Ateneo Grand Splendid](https://turismo.buenosaires.gob.ar/en/otros-establecimientos/el-ateneo-grand-splendid-bookstore).
- **Kuala Lumpur (009):** Masjid Jamek stands at the confluence of the Klang and Gombak rivers. [Tourism Malaysia: Masjid Jamek](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore/masjid-jamek), [River of Life: Masjid Jamek](https://rolkl.jwp.gov.my/en/posts/masjid-jamek).
